The three user settings

Larger text. Body 21, small 18, captions 16. Bubbles and cards grow taller, never truncate. Chips wrap to two rows.

Higher contrast (AAA). Muted text becomes ink, teal becomes teal-dark (8.8:1), borders become 2px ink, tints become white, badges become ink with white text.

Less motion. Thinking dots stop and the words “Checking your program” carry the meaning. Sheets appear without sliding.

Rules held on every screen

Patients are shown as a code such as P-021. No names, photos or emails.

Tap targets 44px or larger. Most are 48.

Status is always words plus shape: “2 new”, “Cancelled”, “Past”, “Done for today”.

Nothing written by AI reaches a patient without the therapist, except the coach, which the therapist turns on.

Components and their states

Plan switcher. Active plan, other plan with “N new”, no unread, past plan (archive icon). Wraps in Larger text.

Message bubble. Therapist, you, with photos, with PDF, sending, sent with time, failed with Try again.

Attachment tray. Empty, 1 to 4 photos, 4 of 4 (add disabled, says why), 1 PDF (photos disabled), uploading, remove on each.

Add menu. Take a photo (phones only), Choose photos, Choose a PDF.

Compose box. Empty (send disabled), typing, privacy warning, sending. Hidden for past plans.

Privacy warning. Phone number found, name found. Actions: Edit message, Send anyway.

AI label. Answer label, chat banner, AI-drafted reply, AI-drafted summary.

Coach answer. Thinking, answer with source link, referral card, emergency card.

Referral card. Default, after tap (“Your question is in a new message”).

Emergency card. One state. Replaces any AI text. Call 911 and Find an emergency room.

Coach unavailable. Turned off, daily limit reached, cannot connect. Each links to Messages.

Plan card. To do, in progress, done for today, coach entry.

Appointment row. Upcoming, next, reschedule requested, cancelled (struck through), past.

Month view day. Plain, today (ring), visit (filled), cancelled (dashed and struck), past.
